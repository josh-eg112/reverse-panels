# Privacy

Reverse Panels does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.

- **No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.** The app contains no third-party SDKs.
- **Network access** goes only to the servers and storage you add: Kavita, OPDS catalogs,
  Nextcloud, and iCloud Drive through iOS.
- **Credentials** (Kavita Auth Keys, Nextcloud app passwords) are stored in the iOS Keychain on
  your device. They are device-only and never synced to iCloud Keychain.
- **Reading progress, favorites, and downloads** are stored on your device or on your own server.
- **Panel detection** runs entirely on your device. Pages are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Panel training tools** (Settings → Internal Training Tools) are off by default. If you use them,
  corrections stay on your device, and an export goes only to a folder you choose.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/josh-eg112/reverse-panels/issues.
